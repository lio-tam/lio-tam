# Lio Tam

I'm a Computer Engineering student at RWTH Aachen and will finish my bachelor's in spring 2027. Before that I studied medicine for two years at Queen's University Belfast. My thesis is where the two meet; most of my other projects are embedded systems.

### Projects

- **[Satellite-Tracker-STM32](https://github.com/lio-tam/Satellite-Tracker-STM32)**: a two-axis mount that follows the ISS in real time. My part was the coordinate conversion in C (WGS-84 → local East-North-Up → azimuth/elevation).
- **[Autonomous-SIMA-Robot](https://github.com/lio-tam/Autonomous-SIMA-Robot)**: motor-driver PCB in KiCad and STM32 bring-up firmware in C++ (modm) for a Eurobot robot at the Roboter Club Aachen.
- **[hackathon](https://github.com/lio-tam/hackathon)**: PID controller for a self-balancing robot from the Infineon challenge at EESTEC 2025 (Arduino, C++).
- **[Full-Stack-Open-2023](https://github.com/lio-tam/Full-Stack-Open-2023)**: my exercises from the University of Helsinki's Full Stack Open course, parts 0 to 3 (React, Node.js, MongoDB).

My bachelor's thesis isn't public because it uses clinical data. I compared Gaussian processes and quantile regression forests with autoregressive models for forecasting the insulin sensitivity of ICU patients, in MATLAB and Python.
